Overview

Two features, one principle

🎁 Lucky Chest

A daily treasure-box board that lives inside every game via a floating icon. Players earn chances to open prize boxes simply by playing — every wager counts toward the next unlock.

Goal: give players a reason to come back every day and keep playing across games.

🎟️ Free Spin Card

A voucher for N free spins on a curated list of games, held in the player's in-game backpack. Winnings accumulate on the card and are settled as real cash in one shot.

Goal: cross-promote games and reward players with tangible, spendable value.

The key principle: every reward paid by these features is funded from inside the game's RTP allocation — it is a reallocation, not an add-on.

Game RTP + campaign rewards = 96.7%, always.
Your revenue share and the game's payout profile are unaffected.

Delivery model

Rewards are credited as real cash to the player's main wallet — instantly withdrawable, no bonus wallet, no wagering requirement. Every payout is a bet ticket you can audit.

Lucky Chest · Concept

A daily 9-box prize board, driven by real play

What the player sees

A floating chest icon sits in every game. Tapping it opens the Game Center, where Lucky Chest is one tap away — no app switching, no separate lobby.

The daily board

Each day the board holds 9 prizes: 5 small, 2 medium, 1 large, 1 final jackpot. Prizes are first shown face-up, then shuffled and turned face-down.

Earning flips

As the player wagers in any covered game, their cumulative bet crosses thresholds — each threshold grants one flip, and the player freely picks which box to open.

Prize amounts and thresholds are fully configurable per campaign and player segment from the back office.

Free Spin Card · Concept

A free-spins voucher the player actually owns

What it is

One card = N free spins playable across a curated list of games (up to 6). Cards come in 1★–5★ tiers, each tier mapping to a bet size per spin and a win cap.

Locked at issuance

Currency, validity window, game list and win cap are frozen the moment the card is issued — what the player sees in the backpack is exactly what settles.

One shared spin pool

Spins are shared across the card's game list: play 3 spins in game A, switch and use the rest in game B. This makes the card a natural cross-game discovery tool.

Cards are granted by Lucky Chest (phase 2), campaigns, or manual back-office issuance.

Free Spin Card · Settlement

One card, one settlement, real cash

1
Trigger — spins used up, card expires, or player cashes out early after hitting the cap (whichever comes first)
▼
2
Total the card's winnings — every spin's win was accumulated on the card, wallet untouched so far
▼
3
Apply the win cap — final payout = min(accumulated winnings, card cap)
▼
4
Pay as one Bet = 0 ticket → main wallet, real cash, instantly withdrawable — same auditable rail as Lucky Chest

Why settle once?

A single settlement keeps the win cap enforceable, produces exactly one clean ledger entry per card, and avoids per-spin payout traffic across systems.
